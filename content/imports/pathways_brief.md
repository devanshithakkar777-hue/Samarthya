For SAMARTHYA, I would approach this as a **comprehensive Indian Education & Career Pathways Directory** rather than a simple list of Science, Commerce and Arts.

The directory should cover education from Class 10 onwards, including traditional academic streams, vocational and technical education, professional qualifications such as CA and CFA, specialised certifications, research degrees and emerging interdisciplinary fields.

One important distinction: **an educational stream, a degree, a professional qualification and a certification are not the same thing.** For example, Commerce is a stream, B.Com is a degree, CA is a professional qualification, and CFA is a professional designation. SAMARTHYA should distinguish these while connecting them in one searchable system.

I'll use the Indian education system as the primary framework, while including internationally recognised qualifications available to Indian learners.

# **1. The major educational streams in India**

The first thing to understand is that the traditional four-stream classification is no longer sufficient. Modern education includes specialised, vocational, interdisciplinary and professional pathways, many of which are open to students from more than one Class 12 stream.

For SAMARTHYA, I recommend organising the directory into these **15 broad educational streams**.

## **A. School-level and foundational pathways**

| Stream                  | Main subjects / areas                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Science — PCM        | Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics                                                                      |
| 2. Science — PCB        | Physics, Chemistry, Biology                                                                          |
| 3. Science — PCMB       | Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology                                                             |
| 4. Commerce             | Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Mathematics, Entrepreneurship                              |
| 5. Humanities / Arts    | History, Political Science, Geography, Sociology, Psychology, Philosophy, Languages                  |

These are broad discovery categories, not mutually exclusive academic classifications. For example, economics belongs to both humanities and commerce-related pathways, while biotechnology connects science, engineering and healthcare.

## **6. What information every course or qualification should contain**

This is the most important part of making the directory genuinely useful.

Every entry should have:

1. **Qualification name:** Full name and commonly used abbreviation.
2. **Category:** Stream, subject and specialisation.
3. **Qualification type:** Degree, diploma, professional qualification, certification, licence or examination.
4. **Awarding authority:** University, statutory body, professional institute or certification provider.
5. **Eligibility:** Class 12 subjects, minimum marks, age restrictions and prior qualifications, where applicable.
6. **Entry route:** Entrance examination, direct admission, professional foundation examination or other route.
7. **Duration:** Expected duration and mandatory training.
8. **Syllabus:** Subjects, papers and practical components.
9. **Recognition:** Regulatory approval, accreditation, registration or international recognition.
10. **Cost and financial assistance:** Publicly available fee information, scholarships and free preparation resources.
11. **Career pathways:** Roles and sectors accessible through the qualification.
12. **Further education:** Higher qualifications and specialisations.
13. **Alternative pathways:** Other ways to enter the same profession.
14. **Official links:** Institution, awarding authority, examination and application portals.
15. **Last verified date:** To prevent outdated information from misleading students.

For example, someone exploring CA should be able to see the ICAI pathway, eligibility, Foundation, Intermediate, practical training, Final and membership requirements. Someone exploring CFA should see its separate eligibility, examination levels, practical work requirements and charter requirements.

## **My recommendation for SAMARTHYA**

Build this as a **living national qualification database**, not a one-time article.

There are thousands of degree programmes, specialisations, vocational qualifications and professional credentials. The catalogue above covers the major pathways, but it is not an exhaustive enumeration of every programme offered by every institution.

I would structure the database around three interconnected questions:

- **What can I study?** — Streams, subjects, degrees, diplomas and professional qualifications.
- **How can I enter?** — Eligibility, prerequisites, entrance exams, alternative routes and scholarships.
- **Where can it take me?** — Careers, industries, further education, licensing and professional progression.

That last connection is especially important: a student should be able to start with an interest such as finance, robotics, psychology, law or public policy and discover all the legitimate educational routes available to reach it.

**The goal should be that no student misses an opportunity simply because they did not know that a particular course, qualification or career pathway existed.**
